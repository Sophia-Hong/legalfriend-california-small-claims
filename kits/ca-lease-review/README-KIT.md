# LegalFriend — California Lease Review Kit

Thank you for your purchase. This kit is a self-help publication for California renters. It
helps you and **the AI you choose** read your lease section by section, spot clauses worth a
closer look under California law, and keep clear records from move-in to move-out.

> Before you start, read `DISCLAIMER.md`. Buying or using this kit does not create an
> attorney-client relationship. LegalFriend never sees your lease, your prompts, or your AI's
> answers. The kit gives general information, not advice about your specific lease.

## What's inside

| Folder / file | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ebook/` | The guide, one chapter per topic: before you sign, money, rules and clauses, repairs, privacy and entry, ending the lease, and getting your deposit back |
| `knowledge/all-in-one.md` | The ebook, practice notes, and facts in one file — the easiest thing to upload to an AI project |
| `sources/facts.yaml` | Every rule the kit states, each with its citation |
| `sources/manifest.yaml` | Official sources: titles and links |
| `practice-notes/` | LegalFriend's general tips and common pitfalls, kept separate from official sources |
| `prompts/` | Instructions for your AI: `system.md` plus task prompts (lease extraction, clause check, questions for the landlord, move-in record, deposit return) |
| `workflows/` | The lease review steps in order, with the facts and sources behind each step |

## Set it up in 5 minutes

1. Open your AI tool and create a new **project** (or custom assistant) just for your lease.
2. Paste the contents of `prompts/system.md` into the project's **instructions**.
3. Upload `knowledge/all-in-one.md` as project **knowledge**. (You can upload the separate
   folders instead if your tool handles many files well.)
4. Share your lease, then paste `prompts/lease-extraction.md` and say: *"Please extract my
   lease section by section."* Follow with `prompts/clause-check.md`.
5. When your AI states a rule, deadline, or limit, click through to the official link it
   gives and confirm it. Laws change, and many cities have their own rules too.

Chapter 1 of the ebook covers this in more detail.

## Before you share your lease

Your AI provider's own terms govern what happens to what you share with it. Before uploading
or pasting your lease, you may want to cover or remove:

- Social Security numbers and dates of birth
- Bank account, card, or payment details
- Signatures

The kit works fine with these redacted.

## Reading the labels

- **[OFFICIAL SOURCE]** — what California law says, from the kit's facts sheet, with a link.
- **[LEGALFRIEND PRACTICE NOTE]** — general tips from LegalFriend. Not advice about your lease.
- **[AI WORKSPACE]** — what your AI produced for you: extractions, tables, arithmetic, drafts. Check it.

When your AI calls a clause "worth a closer look," that means it connects to a California rule
and is worth a question — not that anyone has decided it is unenforceable.
