# LegalFriend — California Small Claims Plaintiff Kit

Thank you for your purchase. This kit is a self-help publication built around official
California court materials, organized so you and **the AI you choose** can use them.

> Before you start, read `DISCLAIMER.md`. Buying or using this kit does not create an
> attorney-client relationship, and LegalFriend never receives your case information.

## What's inside

| Folder / file | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ebook/` | The guide, one chapter per step, from "is small claims right?" to collecting a judgment |
| `knowledge/all-in-one.md` | The whole kit in one file — the easiest thing to upload to an AI project |
| `sources/facts.yaml` | Every rule the kit states, each with its citation |
| `sources/manifest.yaml` | Official sources: titles, links, and version information |
| `practice-notes/` | LegalFriend's general tips and common pitfalls, kept separate from official sources |
| `prompts/` | Instructions for your AI: `system.md` plus task prompts (timeline, SC-100, evidence, hearing) |
| `workflows/` | The plaintiff workflow step by step, with the facts and sources behind each step |

## Set it up in 5 minutes

1. Open your AI tool and create a new **project** (or custom assistant) just for your case.
2. Paste the contents of `prompts/system.md` into the project's **instructions**.
3. Upload `knowledge/all-in-one.md` as project **knowledge**. (You can upload the separate
   folders instead if your tool handles many files well.)
4. Start with: *"I'm the plaintiff. Help me work through the plaintiff workflow, starting with
   whether small claims fits my dispute."*
5. When your AI states a rule, deadline, or form, click through to the official link it gives
   and confirm it. Forms, fees, and rules change.

Chapter 12 of the ebook covers this in more detail, including privacy tips for what you share
with your AI provider.

## Reading the labels

- **[OFFICIAL SOURCE]** — what California Courts, the Judicial Council, or the law says, with a link.
- **[LEGALFRIEND PRACTICE NOTE]** — general tips from LegalFriend. Not advice about your case.
- **[AI WORKSPACE]** — what your AI produced for you: timelines, worksheets, drafts. Check it.
