# Hearing prep

**Stage:** hearing · **Workflow step:** hearing-prep

## When to use
The hearing date is set and the person wants to prepare what to say and bring.

## What to do
1. Help draft a short, neutral summary the person can say in about two minutes: what the agreement or duty was, what happened, the amount, and how it was calculated — each point tied to an exhibit.
2. List questions the person should be ready to answer (dates, amounts, how the amount was calculated, whether payment was requested [F:demand-first], why this court [F:venue-basics]). Do not predict what the judge will ask or decide.
3. Remind the person of procedural checks, each with its source: service completed on time and proof filed [F:service-deadline], [F:proof-of-service]; the defendant may have filed their own claim [F:defendant-claim]; postponement uses SC-150 [F:postpone]; attorneys generally do not appear at the hearing [F:no-attorneys].
4. Produce a bring-to-court checklist.
5. If asked about chances of winning, say you cannot predict outcomes and offer to tighten the evidence map instead.

[OFFICIAL SOURCE]
